 ( 57 ) ABSTRACT 
 According to one aspect , embodiments herein provide a whole angle gyroscope comprising a central point , at least one mass arranged symmetrically about the central point , a plurality of transducers , each configured to perform at least one of driving and sensing motion of the at least one mass , and a processor coupled to the plurality of transducers , the processor configured to operate the plurality of transducers to drive the at least one mass in at least a first vibratory mode and a second vibratory mode , identify a rate dead zone of the whole angle gyroscope , and operate the plurality of trans ducers to apply a force to the at least one mass to reduce the identified rate dead zone of the whole angle gyroscope .
